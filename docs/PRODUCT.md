# Baki GO — Product

## Purpose

Baki GO is **not** a CRM.

Baki GO is **not** a KPI Dashboard.

Baki GO is **not** a task management tool.

Baki GO is a **Business Operating System** designed specifically for **Network Marketing organizations**.

Its mission is to help every member know exactly what they should do next, every single day.

## Golden Rule

> Every member should know exactly what to do next.

## Core Mission

Guide every member through clear goals, automatic tracking, and gamified progression:

```
New Member
    ↓
Supervisor
    ↓
Active Supervisor
    ↓
World Team
    ↓
President
```

## What Baki GO Is

- A daily action guide for network marketing members
- A motivation engine that makes progress visible and rewarding
- A leader cockpit for coaching based on real activity
- A meeting-ready view of team progress

## What Baki GO Is Not

- A generic CRM with contacts and pipelines
- A static dashboard of KPIs nobody acts on
- A complicated tool that requires CRM training to use

## Target Users

| User | Need |
|------|------|
| **Members** | Clear daily next steps, visible progress toward rank |
| **Leaders / Supervisors** | Instant view of who needs help, who is improving, who is falling behind, who deserves recognition |
| **Teams** | Shared progress review during weekly meetings |

## Product Principles

### 1. Mobile First

Everything must be designed for phones first. Desktop is secondary.

### 2. Enter Data Once

Users should never enter the same information twice. Every statistic must be calculated automatically.

### 3. Motivation First

Every screen should increase motivation. Every interaction should make users want to come back tomorrow.

### 4. Business Before Technology

Business rules always come first. Technology exists only to support the business.

### 5. Simplicity

The product should be usable by someone who has never used CRM software. If a feature feels complicated, redesign it.

### 6. Gamification

Progress should feel like a game. Members earn levels, experience, badges, achievements, titles, and rankings. Every important milestone should have visual feedback.

### 7. Meeting Friendly

The product should naturally support weekly meetings. Members should be able to review progress together on one screen.

### 8. Leader First

Leaders should instantly know: who needs help, who is improving, who is falling behind, and who deserves recognition.

### 9. Data Driven

All coaching should be based on real activity. Never rely on memory.

### 10. Long-Term Thinking

Every feature should still make sense five years from now. Avoid shortcuts. Build systems instead.

## Career Path

| Rank | Description |
